# FirmiCore – Facebook Video 1: Triage Guide – "The 2 AM Breakdown"

**Length:** 30 s = 3 clips × 10 s, one continuous story. **Aspect:** 9:16 (export 4:5 for the feed). **Sound-off:** burn in captions.
**Marketing point:** when a machine stops, the first minutes decide the loss. Triage gives anyone a safe, clear, step-by-step path to the fix.

## Keep characters and environment identical
1. Paste the LOCK BLOCK at the start of all 3 clip prompts, unchanged.
2. Generate Clip 1. Use its **last frame** as the first-frame / image reference for Clip 2, then Clip 2's last frame for Clip 3.
3. Same model, seed, aspect ratio and lens wording for all 3.
4. Don't let the AI draw the app UI. Replace the phone screen with a real FirmiCore screen recording in editing. Add logo, captions and CTA in editing too.

**LOCK BLOCK:**
`Nimal, 35-year-old Sri Lankan maintenance technician, short black hair, trimmed moustache, navy coverall with orange reflective stripes, white hard hat, black safety boots, calm expression. Set: night shift in a bottling plant, blue-and-steel filling machine with a yellow "3" sign, grey epoxy floor, green railings, cool white lights, amber warning beacon.`

**Style (append to every prompt):** `cinematic commercial look, natural industrial lighting, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, warm skin tones, clean realistic factory, no text, no logos, no subtitles, consistent character and set, smooth camera move`
